## The entry

One sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance — the endless shower:

> A showerhead that only turns hot after you solemnly promise it, out loud, that this shower will be under five minutes — and turns ice-cold the instant you start singing.

The invention is absurd (a showerhead that takes a verbal vow), the fix is silly yet weirdly plausible (it enforces short showers by holding the hot water hostage, then punishes shower-singing with an ice-cold blast), and the annoyance is one everyone knows: showers that stretch on forever.

## How it works
